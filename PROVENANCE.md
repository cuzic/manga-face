# Provenance of the art

Every part of the art in this repository is original geometry **written as code** (ADR 0001 D6). The parts listed below were authored without generative-model drawings, tracing, or reference matching. Open Peeps and DiceBear are structural references only (the slot layout and the named colour slots); none of their paths is used.

A part is a face outline, an eye kind, a brow, a mouth kind, a nose, a hair template, or a preset. **A PR that adds or changes a part must add or change its row;
`test/provenance.test.ts` fails when a part has no row.**
